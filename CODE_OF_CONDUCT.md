# Code of Conduct

Atlas Music is an open-source project, and everyone participating in its community is expected to help keep it welcoming and respectful.

## Our standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment include:

- Being respectful and constructive when discussing ideas or giving feedback.
- Welcoming different viewpoints and experiences.
- Focusing criticism on ideas, code, and decisions—not people.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, intimidation, and publishing someone else's private information without permission.

## Scope and enforcement

This code applies in project spaces, including issues, pull requests, and discussions, and when someone is representing Atlas Music in public.

Project maintainers may remove or edit contributions and may temporarily or permanently exclude people who violate this code. They will consider the nature and impact of the behavior when deciding how to respond.

## Reporting

Please do not report conduct concerns in a public issue or pull request. Use GitHub's private abuse-reporting process at <https://github.com/contact/report-abuse> and include links and relevant context. GitHub handles these reports under its platform policies.
